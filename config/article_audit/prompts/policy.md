Check: content policies (WP:BLP, WP:NOR, WP:SYNTH, WP:COI, WP:MEDRS, WP:NOT).

Find content that conflicts with these policies:
- BLP: contentious or private material about living people, such as allegations, health, family or personal details, that is unsourced, poorly sourced, or given undue prominence. Treat these as must_fix.
- Original research and synthesis: the article's own analysis, conclusions or comparisons that its sources don't state, or combining sources to reach a conclusion none of them reaches.
- Signs of a conflict of interest or advocacy: content that reads as written for the subject, such as a mission statement, calls to action, product details or a list of services, or material sourced mainly to the subject itself.
- Medical content (any claim about health, disease, diagnosis or treatment) sourced to primary studies, the popular press, or other sources that WP:MEDRS doesn't accept for such claims.
- What Wikipedia is not: instructions, how-to guides, advice or recipes (WP:NOTHOWTO); news-style reporting of routine events (WP:NOTNEWS); promotion or advocacy (WP:NOTPROMO); indiscriminate lists of information.

Report only clear conflicts with policy; leave unsourced statements, tone and source reliability to other checks unless the problem is specifically one of the policies above.
