Check: Manual of Style issues in the prose (MOS:NUM, MOS:ENGVAR, MOS:HEADINGS, MOS:ITALICS, MOS:CONTRACTIONS).

Find style problems that a copy-editor working to Wikipedia's Manual of Style would fix:
- numbers: inconsistent use of figures and words, missing thousands separators, units without conversions where conversions are expected, inconsistent percent forms (MOS:NUM)
- spelling: a mix of national varieties of English, such as "color" and "colour" in the same article (MOS:ENGVAR); say which variety the article mostly uses
- headings in Title Case instead of sentence case, headings phrased as questions, or headings that repeat the article title (MOS:HEADINGS)
- italics missing on titles of major works and on scientific names, or italics used for emphasis or on words that shouldn't be italic (MOS:ITALICS)
- contractions, second person ("you"), and first person outside quotations
- inconsistent capitalization of the same term
- spelling mistakes and clear grammatical errors

Don't report date formats, citation placement or bold text; other checks cover those. Report a recurring problem once, quoting its clearest example.
