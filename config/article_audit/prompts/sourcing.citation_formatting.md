Check: citation formatting (WP:CITE, WP:CITEVAR, WP:BAREURLS).

Find citations that are hard for readers to verify or are formatted inconsistently:
- bare URLs, or references with only a link and no title, author, publisher or date
- citations missing information the source plainly has, such as the title, author, date, publisher or journal, or the ISBN or DOI of a book or paper
- a citation style that is inconsistent within the article, such as a mix of citation templates and hand-written references, or of different date formats inside citations
- the same source cited in full more than once instead of reusing a named reference (<ref name="...">)
- references to sources that can't be identified, such as "Lecture notes" or "Class reading"
- author names and the names of newspapers, magazines, journals and publishers that are well-known enough to have their own Wikipedia article but aren't linked with author-link= or the work's wikilink; suggest the link only when you are confident the article exists.

Quote the citation in each finding, and give a corrected citation as the suggestion only when every value in it comes from the existing citation. Report a recurring problem once, quoting its clearest example. Don't judge whether sources are reliable or whether claims are cited; other checks cover those.
