Check: wikilinks (MOS:LINK, MOS:OVERLINK, MOS:UNDERLINK).

Find:
- underlinking: the first mention of terms a reader may need to understand the article and that are very likely to have their own English Wikipedia article (technical terms, people, places, organizations, events, related concepts), when they aren't linked. Give the linked wikitext as the suggestion, using the title you would expect the target article to have. Prefer specific targets over general ones. Report no more than about ten of the most useful links.
- overlinking: links to common words and widely known places, things and concepts that readers don't need explained, such as countries, major cities, common occupations, languages, or everyday words (MOS:OVERLINK).
- links that probably go to the wrong article, such as a disambiguation page or a different subject with the same name.
- links in section headings (MOS:NOLINKSINHEADINGS).

Don't report a term being linked more than once, or external links; other checks cover those.
